CORE PRINCIPLE

“Recruitment starts with clear job analysis and approved vacancies; selection strictly checks candidates against predefined job specifications.”

Job Analysis (About the Job)

ILLUSTRATION: SECL ASST. MINE MANAGER
  • Key Duties: Supervising shift operations in open-cast mines, managing machinery dispatch, and safety monitoring.
  • Safety Responsibilities: Ensuring strict compliance with DGMS safety rules and the Mines Act 1952.
  • Working Conditions: Rotating shift work, active open pit mining environment, and heavy machinery operations.

Job Specification (About the Person)

CANDIDATE QUALIFICATION BENCHMARK
  • Education: Degree in Mining Engineering (B.E. / B.Tech) from an AICTE recognized institution.
  • Statutory Certification: Valid Mine Manager's Certificate of Competency issued by DGMS.
  • Physical Fitness: High medical and physical fitness standards under Mines Rules 1955.
  • Key Skills: Decision making, safety awareness, leadership, and effective team communication.
🛡️
Medical Fitness Requirement: In mining operations, selection is not only based on marks. Every selected candidate must pass the Initial Medical Examination (IME) under the Mines Rules 1955 before joining.
● 06. UPSTREAM STRATEGY

MANPOWER PLANNING & GAP FORMULA.

DEMAND-DRIVEN STRATEGY SECL MANPOWER MATRIX

4 Core Questions of Manpower Planning

Upstream workforce assessment before any recruitment begins

HOW MANY?

Calculating sanctioned posts vs current working employees across all 13 operating areas.

WHAT SKILLS?

Identifying required technical qualifications, DGMS safety certificates, and job skills.

WHEN NEEDED?

Planning hiring timelines based on upcoming retirements and annual coal production targets.

WHERE DEPLOYED?

Allocating workforce between Bilaspur headquarters and remote opencast or underground mines.

🎯
Core Manpower Principle: Recruitment in a PSU cannot operate in isolation; it must strictly be authorized by approved manpower budgets and sanctioned cadre limits.

Gap Formula & SECL Intake Pathways

Mathematical foundation and actual induction pathways at SECL

MANPOWER GAP FORMULA
Required Workforce − Available Workforce = Manpower Gap

Determines net hiring or redeployment needs based on retirements, expansion, and sanctioned limits.

SECL 4 WORKFORCE INTAKE PATHWAYS (OBSERVED IN PRACTICE)
1. CIL Open MT Exam: Centralized national CBT and campus hiring 2. Internal Promotion: Departmental career progression for existing staff 3. Land Loser Employment: Jobs under state Rehabilitation & Resettlement (R&R) 4. Compassionate Appointment: Dependent jobs under NCWA Clause 9.3.0
● 07. PRACTICAL EXPERIENCES

INTERNSHIP WORK & PRACTICAL LEARNING.

DEPARTMENTAL ROTATION PRACTICAL DESK WORK

Practical Learning & Desk Exposure

Operational HR exposure across documentation, casework, employee relations, and training

1. Documentation & Record Management
  • Service Books & Records: Observed executive service records, leave ledgers, and pay-fixation entries in EE.
  • Transfers & Postings: Studied transfer orders, joining reports, and movement tracking across 13 areas.
  • Document Continuity: Checked note-sheets, arranged papers, and tracked file movement.
2. Understanding Manpower Cases
  • Sanctioned Post Reviews: Compared actual working staff against approved manpower limits.
  • Landowner Employment: Observed employment files under state Rehabilitation & Resettlement (R&R) policy.
  • Compassionate Appointments: Studied dependent employment files processed under NCWA Clause 9.3.0.
3. Employee Relations
  • Management-Union Relations: Observed Joint Consultative Committee meetings and union correspondence in IR.
  • Workplace Harmony: Studied formal grievance handling procedures and dispute resolution methods.
  • Statutory Compliance: Examined welfare and working conditions under the Mines Act 1952.
4. Training & Development
  • Induction Programs: Observed orientation modules for newly joined Management Trainees at MDI Bilaspur.
  • Technical Upskilling: Studied annual training schedules and equipment refresher courses.
  • Safety Certification: Observed mandatory mining safety training compliance under DGMS guidelines.
📁
Practical Learning Takeaway: Observing real files showed that public sector HR relies on careful documentation, official approvals, and clear rules rather than informal decisions.
