RESEARCH PROBLEM STATEMENT

In a mining PSU like SECL, recruitment and selection cannot be treated as routine clerical tasks. They must be closely linked with manpower planning, legal rules, and continuous employee administration after joining.

Why Recruitment & Selection?

🎯
Entry Point of HR: They represent the first step of the employee relationship, setting the foundation for future workforce productivity.
🌊
Building the Candidate Pool: Recruitment announces vacancies publicly to attract a qualified and diverse group of applicants.
⚖️
Evaluating Candidates: Selection evaluates applicants through tests, interviews, and medical checks against predefined job specifications.
⚡
Matching Job Requirements: Proper selection ensures employees meet DGMS mining safety standards, physical fitness, and operational needs.
KEY HR INSIGHT

Careful selection avoids wrong hiring decisions, which is especially important in high-risk mining operations where safety is essential.

Objectives of the Study

  • ✓ Understand Core Concepts: Learn the basic concepts and key differences between recruitment and selection.
  • ✓ Step-by-Step Flow: Study the complete hiring process from vacancy planning to employee induction.
  • ✓ Manpower Planning Role: Examine how manpower planning creates and justifies the need for recruitment.
  • ✓ Department Coordination: Understand how EE, Manpower, IR, and HRD work together in the HR lifecycle.
  • ✓ Practical Exposure: Document real observations of public sector HR files, procedures, and daily work.
  • ✓ Practical Suggestions: Identify practical challenges and suggest useful improvements for SECL.
STUDY METHODOLOGY

Based on practical desk rotation, study of official CIL guidelines, and interaction with experienced HR officers at SECL Bilaspur Headquarters.

06. CONCEPTUAL FOUNDATION

Recruitment & Selection — Concepts & Differences

Attraction vs Evaluation
POSITIVE / ATTRACTION PROCESS

Recruitment

MAIN PURPOSE

To announce job vacancies and build a qualified pool of candidates.

Approach: Positive & Open: Aims to attract eligible candidates across various media channels.
Main Objective: Building Candidate Pool: Ensures sufficient competition and choice for open jobs.
Methods & Channels: Outreach Media: Employment News, official CIL/SECL websites, national newspapers, and campus drives.
Process Order: First Phase: Starts after Manpower Planning approves a vacancy requirement.
Impact of Error: Low Impact: Getting more applications increases clerical work, but does not affect mine safety.
KEY INSIGHT ON VOLUME

A large number of applicants does not automatically mean high quality. Clear Job Specifications are necessary to attract the right candidates.

MEASURABLE OUTPUT Qualified Pool of Applicants
VS
NEGATIVE / SCREENING PROCESS

Selection

MAIN PURPOSE

To evaluate candidates and choose the most suitable person through tests and interviews.

Approach: Filtering & Evaluative: Aims to eliminate applicants who do not meet required standards.
Main Objective: Matching Person with Job: Selects candidates who have the required skills, physical fitness, and qualifications.
Methods & Tools: Structured Assessment: Computer Based Tests (CBT), personal interviews, document verification, and Initial Medical Examination (IME).
Process Order: Second Phase: Follows recruitment and transfers selected candidates to Executive Establishment.
Impact of Error: High Impact: A wrong hiring decision in mining can compromise safety and production.
RULES & MERITOCRACY

In a public sector enterprise like SECL, selection must follow strict merit, government reservation rules (SC/ST/OBC/EWS), and medical fitness standards.

MEASURABLE OUTPUT Selected & Appointed Employees
⚖️
The Core HR Principle: “Recruitment attracts the candidate pool; Selection chooses the best person from that pool.” Both steps are necessary to find the right talent.

Step-by-Step Recruitment Process

Standard public sector hiring pipeline from initial vacancy planning to employee onboarding

CORE PRINCIPLE

“Recruitment starts with clear job analysis and approved vacancies; selection strictly checks candidates against predefined job specifications.”

Job Analysis (About the Job)

ILLUSTRATION: SECL ASST. MINE MANAGER
  • Key Duties: Supervising shift operations in open-cast mines, managing machinery dispatch, and safety monitoring.
  • Safety Responsibilities: Ensuring strict compliance with DGMS safety rules and the Mines Act 1952.
  • Working Conditions: Rotating shift work, active open pit mining environment, and heavy machinery operations.

Job Specification (About the Person)

CANDIDATE QUALIFICATION BENCHMARK
  • Education: Degree in Mining Engineering (B.E. / B.Tech) from an AICTE recognized institution.
  • Statutory Certification: Valid Mine Manager's Certificate of Competency issued by DGMS.
  • Physical Fitness: High medical and physical fitness standards under Mines Rules 1955.
  • Key Skills: Decision making, safety awareness, leadership, and effective team communication.
🛡️
Medical Fitness Requirement: In mining operations, selection is not only based on marks. Every selected candidate must pass the Initial Medical Examination (IME) under the Mines Rules 1955 before joining.
08. UPSTREAM STRATEGY

Manpower Planning & Recruitment Linkage

Demand-Driven Strategy SECL Manpower Matrix

4 Core Questions of Manpower Planning

HOW MANY?

Calculating sanctioned posts vs current working employees across all 13 operating areas.

WHAT SKILLS?

Identifying required technical qualifications, DGMS safety certificates, and job skills.

WHEN NEEDED?

Planning hiring timelines based on upcoming retirements and annual coal production targets.

WHERE DEPLOYED?

Allocating workforce between Bilaspur headquarters and remote opencast or underground mines.

WORKFORCE GAP FORMULA
Sanctioned Posts − [Current Staff − Retirements] = Net Hiring Need

Takes into account upcoming retirements, transfers, and new mining projects.

SECL 4 WORKFORCE INTAKE PATHWAYS (OBSERVED IN PRACTICE)
1. CIL Open MT Exam: Centralized national CBT and campus hiring 2. Internal Promotion: Departmental career progression for existing staff 3. Land Loser Employment: Jobs under state Rehabilitation & Resettlement (R&R) 4. Compassionate Appointment: Dependent jobs under NCWA Clause 9.3.0
📊
The Strategic Connection: Manpower planning identifies the actual workforce need; without it, recruitment can lead to extra manpower costs or sudden staff shortages.

How HR Responds to Different Manpower Situations

Matching workforce needs with the right HR solutions

Workforce Situation (SECL Context)
Recommended HR Action
Deficit in Numbers Shortage of Employees

Retirements happen faster than new entries in mining and electrical supervisory cadres.

External Recruitment / Internal Transfer

Start CIL open recruitment notification or transfer qualified supervisors from closing pits.

Skill Gap Skill / Technical Shortage

Modernization requires Highwall mining, Surface Miner operations, and ERP software skills.

Training at MDI Bilaspur / Technical Hiring

Organize practical training at MDI Bilaspur or recruit specialized technical professionals.

Workforce Surplus Surplus Workers in Older Mines

Older underground mines closing down lead to extra workers without active roles.

Internal Redeployment & Retraining

Shift workers to expanding opencast mines (like Gevra) after providing machinery training.

Long-Term Growth Upcoming Leadership Vacancies

Upcoming retirement of senior General Managers over the next 3 to 5 years.

Succession Planning & Mentorship

Prepare promising managers through rotational experience across Area offices and mines.

💡
Planning Insight: In a large enterprise like SECL, recruitment is only one solution. Training, promotions, and redeployment are also used to meet workforce needs.

10. CRITICAL ANALYSIS

Challenges & Key Findings

Theory vs Practical Reality

Practical Challenges During Training

01
Large Organisation Scale: Understanding the HR operations of ~42,000 employees across 13 operating areas within a 30-day rotation required quick learning and focus.
02
Different Rules for Different Wings: Each HR wing follows specific rules: CIL Executive Rules in EE, NCWA agreements in IR, and DGMS mining safety standards in HRD.
03
Confidential Employee Information: Personal files, vigilance reports, and performance appraisals are confidential, so I focused on understanding the standard procedures.
04
Theory vs. Public Sector Reality: Textbooks show recruitment as a simple step-by-step process, but in a PSU it involves committee approvals, reservation rules, and trade union discussions.
05
Confidential Internal Data: Internal candidate numbers and exact manpower figures are protected, so my project focuses on qualitative workflow analysis and official procedures.
🔬
Research Approach: Because internal personnel data is confidential, this study uses a qualitative approach based on file workflows, official circulars, and discussions with HR officers.

Major Findings & HR Insights

1

Demand-Driven Recruitment: Recruitment does not start on its own; it begins only after manpower planning confirms sanctioned vacancies approved by CIL.

2

Attraction vs. Screening: Recruitment focuses on attracting candidates (positive process), while selection focuses on screening and choosing the right person (negative process).

3

Continuous Employee Records: Once selection is complete, Executive Establishment maintains the employee's service records throughout their career.

4

Vital Role of Industrial Relations: IR builds trust between management and trade unions to maintain workplace harmony and mine productivity.

5

Practical Training at MDI: Training at MDI Bilaspur prepares new employees for real mining conditions and safety requirements.

6

Department Coordination: All HR wings depend on each other. Delays in manpower planning delay hiring, and incomplete EE records affect promotions.

7

Fairness & Confidentiality: Maintaining confidential personnel records and ensuring fair reservation policies are essential HR duties.

💡
Key Analytical Takeaway: Public sector HR works effectively through standardized rules and transparent procedures, which maintain employee trust and long-term stability.

HR Metrics for Measuring Recruitment Success

Key measures to evaluate how well the recruitment process works

HIRING SPEED
Time-to-Fill
Number of days taken from vacancy approval to candidate joining.
PSU Standard: 120–150 Days • Goal: Reduce time taken for testing and document checks
COMPETITION LEVEL
Selection Ratio
Ratio of selected candidates compared to the total number of applicants.
CIL MT Exam: ~1:85 Ratio • High competition ensures quality engineering talent
OFFER ACCEPTANCE
Joining Ratio
Percentage of selected candidates who actually join the company.
SECL Standard: 90%–94% • High job security and benefits encourage candidates to join
VACANCY TIME
Vacancy Ageing
How long critical mine manager and safety positions remain unfilled.
Target: Under 60 Days • Avoids safety and operational delays in mines
EMPLOYEE RETENTION
First-Year Attrition Rate
Percentage of new employees who leave the organisation during their first year.
SECL Standard: Very Low (<3.5%) • Supported by strong job security, medical, and housing benefits
📈
Future HR Direction: Moving from simple record-keeping to proactive workforce planning—predicting retirements up to two years ahead so hiring starts before positions become vacant.

Suggestions for the Organisation (SECL)

  • Integrated Digital HR Database: Connect employee records across EE, Manpower, and HRD into a single digital system for faster processing.
  • Forward-Looking Manpower Planning: Use retirement forecasts to start the recruitment process early and avoid vacant positions.
  • Need-Based Training at MDI: Design induction training programs based on specific skill needs identified during the selection exam.
  • Digital Reservation Rosters: Maintain digital reservation registers (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) to speed up promotions and ensure transparent compliance.

Suggestions for Future Internships
